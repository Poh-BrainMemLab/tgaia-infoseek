# Repository for 'Trait curiosity shapes epistemic focus and exploration in naturalistic student-GenAI interactions'

Due to privacy and confidentiality considerations, we are unable to share the raw data containing individual student-chatbot exchanges. All preprocessing and acquisition of student-chatbot measures (i.e., semantic distances & annotations) scripts and system prompts are made available for viewing (data_acquisition). The de-identified, processed data required to reproduce the findings of this study can be accessed from our repository (processed_data). Finally, our results script has also been made available (main_analysis).
