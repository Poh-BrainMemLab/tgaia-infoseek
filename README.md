# Repository for 'Trait curiosity shapes epistemic focus and exploration in naturalistic student-GenAI interactions'

Due to privacy and confidentiality considerations, we are unable to share the raw data containing individual student-chatbot exchanges. 

- All preprocessing and acquisition of student-chatbot measures (i.e., semantic distances & annotations) scripts and system prompts are made available for viewing (data_acquisition).
- The de-identified, processed data required to reproduce the findings of this study can be accessed from our repository (processed_data).
- Finally, our results script has also been made available (main_analysis).


# How to read
<b> data_acquisition </b>

- system_prompts: exact prompts used to prompt engineer LLMs to act as classifiers for the dimensions of Agency, Intent and Scope

- preprocessing:

  - 1A_preproces_chatlogs: text cleaning, duplicate management, etc. for raw user-chatbot exchanges

  - 1B_preprocess_chatlogs: script to remove excel artefacts

  - 2_preprocess_acads: imputation of academic scores to manage missingness

- semantic distance

<b> processed data </b>

- Deidentified datafiles for the different labels generated by Gemini 2.5 Pro. Reasons for labels generated by the LLMs have been appended for interest.
